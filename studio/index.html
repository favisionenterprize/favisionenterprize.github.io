    Questions

    Good to know

    Is it really done by AI?

    We use AI tools to draft wording, create room looks and design faster. Then a person on our team checks and finishes every job before we send it, so you get something correct and ready to use.

    How do I pay?

    Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), GhanaPay (*707# or the GhanaPay app) or Visa / Mastercard. You can also pay 50% now and the rest when your work is ready, pay everything when it's ready, or pay at our Odorkor, Omanjor or Kasoa showroom. Guides are paid before we send them.

    Is the room designer really free?

    Yes. Design as many rooms as you like and share them with family. You only pay when you order furniture or wallpaper, and pieces marked "Quote" are priced for you on WhatsApp before you pay anything. With any order we can also show your new pieces in a photo of your real room, free. The bulk setup fee is credited in full when you place the order.

    Can you print my programme, brochure or flyer?

    Yes. F.A Vision runs a printing press at Omanjor. Tell us how many copies you need in your brief and we'll quote printing separately, or call / WhatsApp 020 747 3267 or 054 614 8923.

    What happens to my photos and details?

    We only use them for your job. We don't post your photos or share your details without asking you first.
