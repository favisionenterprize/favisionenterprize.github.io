Questions

Frequently asked

Does F.A Vision Enterprise sell furniture in bulk or wholesale?

Yes. We sell school desks and chairs, office, church, hotel and home furniture in bulk at wholesale prices, with discounts on large orders. Call 020 747 3267, 057 264 6176 or 054 614 8923 for a quote.

Where are F.A Vision Enterprise's shops?

We have three locations: Odorkor (Tarazzo Road, opposite Pacific, Accra), Omanjor (Accra) and Kasoa. We also deliver across Accra, Tema, Kasoa, Kumasi and the rest of Ghana.

Do you do upholstery and sofa re-upholstery?

Yes. We upholster new sofas and chairs to order, and re-cover, re-foam and repair old ones. Send a photo on WhatsApp for a price.

Do you have a bookstore and printing press?

Yes. Our bookstore stocks textbooks, exercise books, school supplies and stationery, and our printing press prints flyers, banners, stickers, business cards and exercise books.

How do I contact F.A Vision Enterprise?

Call or WhatsApp 057 264 6176, or call 020 747 3267 or 054 614 8923. We are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00am to 6:00pm.
